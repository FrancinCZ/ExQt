# About ExQt and me
Hello! My name is Martin Franc, and I am a high school student from Kutná Hora in the Czech Republic. 

ExQt is actually only half of my high school graduation project. I was working with biological expansion microscopy (ExM) in the lab, and a big part of my thesis is highlighting that ExM is an incredibly cheap and affordable way to do super-resolution (SR) microscopy. 

However, I wanted to add an informatics challenge to my work. The idea for ExQt arose when we needed to extract data from our ExM images, and I realized there wasn't a simple software that natively accounted for the physical expansion factor. 

That became my new goal: to build that software myself and learn how to segment images, calculate biological metrics, and tie it all together in Python. I have spent a huge amount of my free time designing and coding ExQt, and it honestly became my passion. 

I am a huge believer in open-source projects, so I wanted to give something new back to the community. Hopefully, this software will help someone out there and make super-resolution microscopy even more accessible to everyone.
